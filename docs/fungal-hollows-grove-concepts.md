# Fungal Hollows and Grove: selected concept directions (#306)

Owner direction, 2026-11-26. This short concept detour precedes returning to
the Cavern's main-path production. Seven selected plates are preserved in
`art/concepts/fungal-hollows-grove-v1`; its `inspector.html` shows the numbered
set. Exact built-in image-generation prompts, input roles, local source paths,
PNG hashes and approvals are in `generation.json`. These are concept images,
not production geometry, final names, poses, encounter rules or item resources.

The owner endorsed the overall set, asked that the Flower Knight be masculine,
then explicitly accepted that revision. Selected boss image is **v2**; v1 is
retained only as a superseded comparison. The separately requested optional
unlockable-character study was subsequently rejected and is **excluded** from
the project concept set. No recruitment character is commissioned.

The unifying material language is damp brown, old ivory, muted plum fungi,
mycelial cords and decayed equipment. The fairy light supplies a gentle contrast;
the boss's unusually immaculate purple bloom signals an exceptional encounter.
Names printed on plates are working art labels. Depicted weapons, spore sacs,
glow, equipment and remains are visual proposals, not gameplay promises.

Keep the previously discussed Fungal Hollows identity in view: faint fungal
light, reduced emphasis on light cost/strain, possible relief at a poison cost,
and numerous poison hazards. Exact useful illumination, costs, cues and
counterplay need gameplay design; these pictures do not modify the current
challenge-family implementation or make every glowing growth poisonous.

The Grove remains a small, exceptionally difficult optional lair. Concealment
must preserve a readable threshold warning and the player's chance to turn
back. Existing Heartrot resources are untouched; reconciling their placeholder
identity, moves and arena with the selected Flower Knight direction is later
integration work. Old `spore_burst/grasp/regrow` suggestions are not automatically
the Flower Knight's approved moves or clips.

All seven selected concepts were generated using the built-in image tool and
copied unchanged into the workspace. User-supplied reference originals are not
vendored into the public repository. These are original new compositions using
the reference roles recorded in the manifest. No private character models,
third-party meshes, external motion or live assignments were introduced.
