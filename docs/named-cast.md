Once the likenesses are selected, author complete eye and mouth replacements, conditions and independent lighting on their own aligned 192-pixel canvases.

Rook's candidate is the existing [Fixer-a PortraitSet](../data/portraits/fixer-v1/fixer-a.tres). It already has a clean head, individually registered neutral features, half and closed blinks, two talk frames, four conditions and light masks. The coat, silver streak and crooked smile match the broker brief. The live recruit pool can already choose this face, so confirm the named mapping and how the pool should reserve it before assigning it to Rook. Current recruits and saves retain their resource paths.
