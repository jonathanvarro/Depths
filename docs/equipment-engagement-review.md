# Equipment engagement: owner feedback, 2026-11-02 (#219)

## Immediate correction

Mastery comes from combat only. Weapon mastery counts successful strikes; armour and shields count landed incoming blows, retaining the existing enemy-threat scaling and per-fight caps. No miss, kill, participation or return-home bonus. Count blows rather than HP lost for this initial correction: losing more health must not accelerate armour progression. Damage-based weapon credit remains a playtest option, not an approved coefficient. Existing mastery is preserved. Back-slot capes/packs have no growing stats in the current tables; this patch does not invent exploration mastery for them.

The drawback substitution kept one normal benefit and replaced the second benefit with a penalty, without compensation. New rolls retain their positive properties until genuinely compensated combinations are designed. Existing saved drawback items retain their effects. This is an interim generation correction, not the final drawback design.

## Equipment sources

Owner wants major theme areas and subareas to have recognisable qualities. Define these before expanding targeted loot. A proposed alternative is a smith slot for each equipment family (for example sword, hammer and spear), with quality and properties rolled daily. Exact family list and number of slots are open. This improves base availability but does not by itself make cave exploration more rewarding; preserve worthwhile cave sources and material hunts. Do not add a daily waiting fee merely to police stock refreshing.

## Growth and comparison

Owner requests simple comparisons beside current stats: equipped value versus candidate value, with an arrow and colour to indicate direction. Use symbols/text alongside red/green so colour alone is not required. Keep accuracy, damage, protection and penalties separate rather than declaring a universal winner.

Owner proposes showing a growth range and rolling a permanent increase within it at each mastery level, instead of the current fixed rate. Show its comparison against worn growth as well. This changes the 2026-10-31 fixed-growth rule; no random-growth implementation is included in #219. First define ranges and rounding, whether outcomes can overlap across tiers, how no-change levels work, how existing items migrate, and how each saved level roll stays fixed across reload/equip/stash. Compare ranges honestly: overlapping ranges are not guaranteed better/worse; compare expected gain and show overlap.

Playtest several runs with mastered ordinary gear, fresh counters and different growth tiers. Measure voluntary switching, decisions players can explain, time spent comparing on a phone, mastery per expedition and return decisions. Earlier paper pace numbers include bonuses now removed and are not current balance claims. Prototype comparison at current mastery and equal mastery before expanding modifier count or art.
