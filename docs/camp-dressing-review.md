# Camp occupancy review · #89

The owner reviewed the clearing and answered: **“A little empty—add subtle signs of people.”** This warrants a small occupancy pass while retaining the six destinations and labels.

## Integrated baseline

The review loaded Main's actual camp with available recruits, animated fire, recovery state and existing audio player. This was not only a composition image. The baseline report records three candidates, advancing fire frames and an active ambience stream: [`camp-before.json`](../art/concepts/campaign-v1/camp-before.json).

The stream is still the cave placeholder. Playback was verified programmatically; its subjective warmth or quality was **not** assessed by listening. Dedicated camp ambience remains #54. Owner feedback is one person's assessment, not broader player testing or a physical-phone trial.

## Small occupancy pass

Four reusable transparent 64px assets in `art/camp/occupancy-v1/`: two quiet seated figures seen from behind, a simple meal and drying cloth. Up to two figures appear according to the actual available recruit count; the meal disappears when none remain. They introduce no extra NPC identities, arrivals, stock or simulation. The cloth remains a restrained sign of occupation.

A separate local warm light affects the clearing, fire base and cues. Its artwork light mask excludes text and controls. The scene remains grey, poor and worn. Existing merchant, nurse and smith art is preserved.
