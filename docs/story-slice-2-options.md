# Story, slice 2: options to choose from

Prepared 2026-10-22 so the owner can set direction with a few taps. Each question has two or three options; ★ marks Claude's recommendation. Decisions go to #122 and DESIGN.md.

**What's already decided** (DESIGN.md, *Story direction*):
- **The setting:** a mine. The miners broke through into something older; gas came first, then monsters; then the goblins took the workings.
- **The relic** is the main goal. The goblin queen is now a side quest.
- **Commander Edric Vael** is stern but fair on the surface. The inspiration is Wizardry's commander, who slowly turns antagonist.
- **The dark elves** are rivals first. Their real aim might be stopping others from misusing the relic.
- **The first slice:** the ambush site, *Three Knocks*, the iron project and the lower water gate.
- **Available tools:** a vision and dream tint, and quest packages in the maze.

---

## 1. What is the relic?
- **★ A seal.** An old stone key that held the breach shut. The miners cracked the wall around it; the gas and the monsters are what leaks through. Whoever holds it can close the breach, or open it wider. This ties the relic straight into the mine's story and the monsters already built.
- **A crown.** The buried city's regalia. Whoever holds it has a claim to the deep, and to the king's own throne.
- **Something alive.** A heart or an egg in the deep that's waking. The darkest horror option.

## 2. Who really wants it, and why?
- **★ The commander, for himself, using the king's name.** The king wants the breach closed. Vael wants the power to open it. This gives a slow turn, like Wizardry's.
- **The king,** to use the breach as a weapon. The betrayal sits higher up and comes late.
- **The king, honestly, to save the realm.** The tension comes from Vael's ruthless methods, not betrayal.

## 3. Who caused the ambush that killed your companions (#107)?
- **★ Goblins, but someone gave them your route.** The map marked "in a stranger's hand" from *Those Left Behind* leads back toward the camp. The truth comes out slowly and is never settled by a single note.
- **Vael directly.** He sent the vanguard as bait, to find a way to the seal.
- **The dark elves.** They're blamed at first, and the player has to decide whether that's true.

## 4. What do the dark elves really want?
- **★ They're the seal's old keepers.** They closed the breach long ago and want the relic back to close it again. Rivals at first, then possible allies.
- **Exiles of the buried city,** who want their home back.
- **Treasure hunters,** and nothing more. They stay rivals to the end.

## 5. What lies below the fortress (floors 5–6)?
- **★ The Drowned Galleries.** The flooded deep mine and the buried city's old waterworks. The proposal (#125) has hooks ready: water routes, a wounded dark elf, and the first city stones.
- **The breach itself.** The gas caverns the monsters came from. More immediate, but it uses up the climax early.
- **A dark-elf outpost.** The rivals' camp, met head-on early.

## 6. How long is the campaign?
- **★ Decide later.** Build floors 5–6 now and see how they play.
- **About 8 floors,** with the seal and the breach at the bottom.
- **12 floors,** as #125 proposes: six layers ending at a temple.

## 7. The first vision (the blue-tint tool)
- **★ The ambush, remembered.** In a dream at camp, the hero relives the moment the vanguard fell, and notices a detail they missed. It's the first clue toward question 3.
- **The miners' breach.** A vision of the gassed shift breaking through, shown when the hero touches the seal's stonework.
- **None yet.** Save visions for later.
