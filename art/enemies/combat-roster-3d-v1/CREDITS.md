# Combat roster source credits

- **Great rat.** [Rat by br-n518](https://opengameart.org/content/rat-0), CC0 1.0. Adapted from the previously verified rat pilot at commit f58c6aa58f98cd62df7012d3060c2c87e6cfcb89. Its original files, source declaration and hashes live in art/third-party/oga-rat-v1 on that branch. Retained the goblin rig, with new clothes, role equipment, palette work and motions. The Queen's crown, skirt and cleaver draw on Depths' existing 2D Queen.
- **Broodmother.** [Spider by br-n518](https://opengameart.org/content/spider-2), CC0 1.0. Adapted from b35345c's web-spinner, with new abdomen proportions, shell ridges, mandibles, palette, call and fall. The original goblin and spider files, declaration pages and hashes are under art/third-party/oga-creatures-v1.
- **Mara, Corin, Sister Alwen and recruit.** Original procedural meshes and animations, authored for Depths. Existing portrait previews are internal references. No private sources are used.
- **Shared grain.** Reuses the existing Depths treatment of the KayKit Dungeon Remastered CC0 textures. Refer to the source record under art/third-party/kaykit-dungeon-remastered.

All new board meshes, equipment and motions are delivered with the source and rebuild paths in docs/combat-roster-3d.md.
