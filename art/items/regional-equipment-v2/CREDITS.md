# Regional equipment production adaptations

Four original built-in ImageGen edits for Depths, 2026-11-22, based on the
project's retained early regional equipment drawings. Exact prompts, edit
targets, source hashes and unmodified generated masters are retained in
`art/source/regional-equipment-v2/generation.json`.

The adaptations fit the existing Quilt coat, Studded jerkin, Goblin scale
shirt and Goblin round shield definitions. Existing IDs, descriptions,
statistics, prices, quality/modifier rolls and regional pools are unchanged.
Earlier sources/icons remain available. Exported directly from each generated
master at 128 and 64 px with the established fitted hard-alpha, padding and
64-colour pipeline. No added rarity frames, emblems, text or glow.

Rebuild with `tools/art/build_regional_equipment_v2.py`. The complete 22-concept
reconciliation is in this folder's catalogue and
`art/concepts/regional-equipment-v2/inspector.html`.
