# Puzzle fittings

Original authored door frame, leaf, locking bar, lock plates, lever fittings
and open-bow key by ChatGPT for Depths, 2026-11-22. The builder reuses the
project's authored mine geometry helpers and worked-prop materials.
No new third-party download or licence was introduced.

The recovered-key scene instances the existing verified CC0 KayKit seated
skeleton from `art/dressing/bone-decor-v1`; original files and licence remain
under `art/third-party/kaykit-bones-v1`. The original key-glint light is retained.
The lever gate reuses `art/props/fortress-waterworks-3d-v1/flow-gate.tscn`.
Neither earlier source was changed by this batch.

Shared material provenance is in `art/materials/worked-props-v2` and
`docs/mine-polish-and-loot-chests.md`. New UVs are physical metres with
vertex colour and UV2 grain controls, not palette-point UVs.

`catalog.json` records triangle counts, bounds and SHA-256 hashes.
Editable separate-piece Blender masters stay local:
`build/local-puzzles-elites-v1/masters/puzzles`.
