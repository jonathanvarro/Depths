# Recurring merchant — progression concept v1

`merchant-progression-v1.png` is a generated three-stage character study for the recurring merchant described in [the design](../../../docs/merchant-design.md). It is meant for review, not runtime use.

- Created: 2026-10-16, using the built-in image-generation tool.
- Original generated output copied into this project without modification; original retained.
- Early, middle and late appearances read left to right. This is one character with stable visual anchors, not three different merchants.
- Working name in the design: Master Tallow (proposal).
- The owner specified a greedy recurring merchant who follows the party between rest locations and becomes increasingly obese and gross. Costume, face, props and the three-stage study are proposed art direction.
- Visually reviewed for progression, consistent identity, dark atmosphere, clothing and composition. It is a flattened concept with three slightly different poses, not precisely registered animation frames or a separated pixel master. Not yet validated as a 192 x 192 game portrait or on iPhone.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: dark fantasy dungeon-crawler recurring merchant character evolution concept sheet, not final game sprites. Create one landscape triptych showing the SAME memorable adult male human merchant at three stages of a campaign, from left to right, with consistent face, pose, camera and costume identity. Knees-up seated behind a rough travel counter, head and both hands visible in each panel, sufficient room for growing silhouette. The merchant follows the party between bleak refuges. Distinctive identifying anchors: small crooked dark-red merchant cap with a brass coin pinned to its brim, thin waxed forked moustache, one conspicuous gold tooth in a calculating ingratiating smile, heavy signet rings, a ledger and tarnished coin scales. Early: stocky, oily, shrewd, patched brocade waistcoat, already unpleasant. Middle: significantly obese, tighter waistcoat, greasy jowls, crumbs and sauce stains, more jewellery. Late: massively obese, swollen layered jowls and belly straining his re-fitted expensive stained clothes, sweaty pallid skin, greasy fingers, rotten-toothed grin with the SAME gold tooth, revolting indulgence and avarice, visibly the SAME man. His personality should remain alert and calculating throughout. Grotesque gothic atmosphere conveyed by predatory smile, grime, gluttonous indulgence and obsessive wealth. Fully clothed, nonsexual, no gore. Detailed expressive low-resolution pixel-art portrait aesthetic, deliberate pixel clusters, restrained charcoal/dirty parchment/rust brown/muted teal palette with warm lantern light from upper left. Simple dark canvas-tent background, no ornate frame, no text or lettering, no labels, no other characters. Keep the three stages visually separate and equally sized, like a production character study. Original design; avoid resemblance to existing game merchant characters.
