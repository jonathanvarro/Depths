# Cave formations and growth

Fourteen metric scenes provide mineral formations, shallow flowstone,
rubble, roots and fungal growth. Mounts and bounds are in catalog.json.
Floor roots anchor at ground level, ceiling pieces at their attachment,
and wall pieces at the centre with local +Z facing into the room.

Fungal glow is off by default. glow_enabled controls emission and
set_motion controls its optional slow pulse or exact still mode.
These are visual decor scenes. Placement and gameplay rules are caller-owned.

Handoff and rebuild steps are in docs/cave-formations-and-growth-3d.md.
