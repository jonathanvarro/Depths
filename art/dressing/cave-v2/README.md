# Remaining cave dressing · #139

Twenty mounted scenery entries: mine fittings, old remains, goblin fixtures,
moss, damp mineral details and deeper stone ornaments. [Catalogue](catalog.json)
records exact textures/scenes, sizes, anchors, families and hashes.
[Inspector](../../concepts/descent-finish-v1/inspector.html) ·
[full mounting/rebuild handoff](../../../docs/descent-surfaces-and-dressing.md).

Transparent 128px, at most 64 visible colours per piece; shaded nearest sprites.
Roots/wall fungus reuse earlier libraries; floor fungus reuses its exact base.
Moss/floor fungus have optional `-lit.tscn` variants with separate registered
eight-frame mood pulses. No light nodes, collision, scripts or interaction.
Overhead planks face down on the ceiling; the drip assembly expects a 1.4 m root.
No live placement or theme assignment. Marking/lore meanings remain unassigned.
