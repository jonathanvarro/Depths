Spider family & goblin base

Low-poly 3D candidates for Depths’ light-aware combat board. The spiders share one rig, with distinct body proportions and broad colour masses. The goblin’s knife and buckler can be removed independently for later equipment variants.

Five paused samples per clip. Front and overhead close show a midpoint pose, or the completed fall. For continuous playback, exact 24fps stepping, still mode, lighting and equipment toggles, use START_REVIEW.cmd in this folder.
