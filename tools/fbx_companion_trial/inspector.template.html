What this proves

The supplied skeleton and idle can support fitted clothes, additional equipment and new seated motion. The same outfit geometry and weights are reused across both appearances. Covered body triangles are hidden; cloth edges use joined offsets to avoid cracks.

The outfit is a fitted shell with added split tunic tails, belt, pouches and field pack. The face details are simple. There is no cloth simulation, foot IK, broader body fitting, expression library, live equipment binding or physical-iPhone benchmark. Motion and likeness remain exploratory.
